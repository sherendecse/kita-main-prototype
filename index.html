Clear rules.
Consistent screening.

Every household is checked against the same criteria.

  1. Malaysian citizen family
  2. Peninsular Malaysia resident
  3. At least one child aged 0–3
  4. Monthly income ≤ RM5,000
  5. STR, eKasih, mySalam or PPR
  6. One allocation per household

How decisions are made

A known failed eligibility condition returns Not qualified, even if other information is missing. If there is no known failure but required information is missing, invalid, or the household is already enrolled or duplicated, return Needs review. Otherwise return Qualified.

Benefit statuses use OR logic: one confirmed STR, eKasih, mySalam or PPR status is sufficient. Income is a separate mandatory condition. Outreach fields never affect the outcome.

Prototype rules based on published criteria as provided for this demo. The precise age cutoff and interpretation of conditions remain subject to YGG confirmation. No live government database verification is performed.
